From our little patch of land

We believe happy hens make a happier home.

Willow & Wing started with a handful of hens, a patch of pasture, and the idea that raising chickens should feel a little more thoughtful. These days, not much has changed.

We raise each bird with room to scratch, sunshine on their backs, and plenty of gentle human company. When they’re ready, they’re ready to bring a little farmyard joy to you.
